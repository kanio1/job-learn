# BPMN, UML and DMN for Testers

## Use when
- modeling business processes,
- modeling interactions,
- modeling object lifecycle,
- turning rules into decision tables.

## When Not to Use
Do not use this for decorative diagrams or problems better solved with short text.

See `.kilocode/skills/bpmn-uml-dmn-for-testers/references/model-to-tests-guide.md`.

# Official Docs and Versioned Research

## Use when
- versions may have changed,
- APIs/configurations are version-sensitive,
- a claim needs primary-source grounding,
- skills/specs must be refreshed.

## When Not to Use
Do not use this for broad exploratory market scans or loose inspiration gathering.

See `.kilocode/skills/official-docs-and-versioned-research/references/research-output-format.md`.

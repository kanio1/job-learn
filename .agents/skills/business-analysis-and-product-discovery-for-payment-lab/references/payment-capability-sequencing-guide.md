# Payment Capability Sequencing Guide

Use as a thinking aid, not a rigid roadmap.

## Suggested progression
1. Foundation
2. First core domain capability
3. Persistence and ownership
4. Identity and authorization
5. Reliability: idempotency, retry, concurrency
6. External integration: PSP simulator, callbacks, webhooks
7. Operations and audit
8. Refunds, settlement and reconciliation

A different order is acceptable when the business narrative and learning value justify it.

# Business Product and Testing Thinking

This area captures business analysis, product discovery, risk thinking, exploratory testing ideas, decision tables, workflow models, and tester-owned analysis that are not tied to one technical note.

Use this area for:

- Business capability discovery before Spec Kit specification.
- Product risks and ambiguity notes.
- Test analysis and design ideas.
- Exploratory charters and debriefs.
- Decision-table, state-transition, BPMN, UML, or DMN thinking notes.

Phase-specific project hubs should link here when business/product/testing thinking becomes durable enough to reuse.
